## What you can do without asking

- **Refer to the project truthfully.** "Built on Poindexter", "a plugin for
  Poindexter", "compatible with Poindexter", "I run Poindexter at home" are
  all fine.
- **Redistribute unmodified releases** under the Poindexter name, for example
  mirrors or distro packages built from an official tag.
- **Write about it.** Blog posts, talks, tutorials, reviews and videos can use
  the name freely.
- **Name a plugin or extension** in the form `<your-name>-for-poindexter` or
  `poindexter-<thing>-plugin`, as long as it is clear the plugin is yours and
  not an official Glad Labs release.

## What needs permission

- **A modified fork shipped under the name.** A fork is welcome, but give it
  its own name. "Based on Poindexter" in its description is fine.
- **A hosted or commercial service called Poindexter** (for example
  "Poindexter Cloud" or "Poindexter Pro" from someone other than Glad Labs).
- **Using the name or logo in a way that suggests Glad Labs endorses,
  sponsors, or supports your product** when it does not.
- **A company, domain, or product name built around "Poindexter"** for
  software or AI services.

## Asking

Email **hello@gladlabs.io** with what you want to do. Reasonable requests from
the community are usually approved.

## Reporting misuse

If you see the Poindexter name used in a way that could confuse people about
who built or supports a product, please tell us at hello@gladlabs.io.
